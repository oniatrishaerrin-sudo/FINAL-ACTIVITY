Educational Background

I took the Electronics track starting from Grade 9 up to Senior High School, where I gained valuable technical knowledge, practical skills, and hands-on experience in the field. Throughout those years, I learned the importance of discipline, precision, problem-solving, and perseverance while working on different technical activities and projects. My dedication and hard work allowed me to successfully complete the program and earn my NC II Certificate, which became one of my proud achievements during my academic journey.

Although I came from a technical background, I realized that I also have interests and goals that align with a different career path. Because of this, I decided to challenge myself and explore new opportunities by pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Office Administration. Choosing a different field was not an easy decision, but I believe that growth comes from stepping outside of one’s comfort zone and being open to new experiences.

Now, as an Office Administration student, I am eager to learn more, develop new knowledge and skills, and apply the discipline, dedication, and work ethic that I gained from my previous educational background. I am confident that my technical experiences, combined with my willingness to learn and adapt, will help me succeed in this new field and prepare me for future opportunities and professional growth.
